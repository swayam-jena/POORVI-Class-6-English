\chapter{RAMA TO THE RESCUE}
\subsection{Let us read}
\textbf{RAMA TO THE RESCUE}-a folktale from Tamil Nadu
\hspace{0.2in}
Script: Luis M. Fernandes\\
Illustrations: Ram Waeerkar
\par
\textit{IN A VILLAGE, ONE NIGHT-}
\par
OH-HUMM! WHAT A HARD DAY IT HAS BEEN!
\par
I'M FEELING SO SLEEPY...
\par
WHAT'S THE MATTER?\\
WHAT ARE YOU LOOKING AT?
\par
LISTEN!
\par
SOMEBODY IS TRYING TO GET IN.
\par
I...I THINK HE HAS ALREADY GOT IN...THE NOISE HAS STOPPED.
\par
LIE DOWN...DON'T LOOK.
\par
IT'S A THIEF...HE MUST HAVE SOMEHOW GOT PAST RAMA.
\par
\textit{RAMA WAS THE VILLAGE HOTWAL.}
\par
WHAT SHOULD WE DO?\\
I'LL TELL YOU. LISTEN...
\par
\textit{MEANWHILE-}
\par
VOICES! THEY'RE AWAKE. I'LL HAVE TO WAIT TILL THEY FALL ASLEEP.
\par
I WONDER WHERE THEY KEEP THEIR MONEY.
\par
THEY'RE SAYING SOMETHING. PERHAPS THEY'RE TALKING ABOUT THEIR MONEY. I'D BETTER LISTEN CLOSELY.
\par
WHAT SHOULD WE MANE OUR CHILD?\\
IF HE IS A BOY WE'LL CALL HIM RAMA.
\par
RAMA? YES...THAT'S GOOD NAME.
\par
WHEN HE'S IN THE HOUSE, I'LL CALL OUT SOFTLY TO HIM, RAMA! RAMA!
\par
BUT WHAT IF HE'S IN THE YARD?\\
THEN I'LL CALL OUT A LITTLE LOUDER, RAMA, RAMA!
\par
I WISH THEY WOULD STOP THIS SILLY GAME AND TALK ABOUT THEIR MONEY INSTEAD.
\par
OR FALL ASLEEP AT LEAST!
\par
BUT MY DEAR, WHAT IF THE BOY IS NOT IN THE HOUSE ON IN THE YARD, BUT IN THE STREET?\\
OH, THEN I'LL CALL OUT VERY LOUDLY...
\par
RAMA! RAMA!
\par
\textit{RAMA THE VILLAGE HOTWAL, RAN TO THE HOUSE FROM WHICH HE HEARD HIS NAME BEING CALLED.}
\par
OH, OH! SOME THIEF HAS DUG HIS WAY INTO THIS HOUSE.
\par
AH, AT LAST THEY'RE STOPPED THEIR CHATTER!
\par
NOW SOON THEY'LL GO TO SLEEP AND ... EH!
\par
\textbf{YOU'RE UNDER ARREST.}
\par
IT'S RAMA! HE HEARD US!\\
THE PLAN WORKED! WE'RE SAVED.
\par
\textit{THE MAN AND HIS WIFE BY THEIR CLEVERNESS HAD SAVED THEMSELVES FROM BEING ROBBED.}
\par
AMAR CHITRA KATHA
